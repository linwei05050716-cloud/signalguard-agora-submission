Short Transcript

SignalGuard watches a prediction market, scores a thesis, applies non-negotiable risk gates, and then either submits a capped action or refuses the trade. The product is the receipt: evidence, limits, status, and proof hash travel together. In the demo, a BUY Up candidate is capped at 1.20 USDC, the submit count is limited to one, and a missing terminal exchange response is surfaced as unresolved risk. The second receipt shows the opposite behavior: when price or edge fails, the agent blocks the action and still emits an auditable receipt. The Arc proof tab shows how the receipt hash and USDC-denominated metadata can be anchored for settlement-ready attribution.
